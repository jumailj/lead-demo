The Admissions Process

A transparent, merit-based selection process for the LEAD MBA 2026 Batch

01

Register & Apply Online

Fill in your application form at admission.lead.ac.in with basic details, academic records, and work experience (if any).

→
02

Entrance Test (CAT / KMAT / MAT / XAT)

Submit valid scores from any national management entrance test. LEAD also accepts Kerala state entrance scores.

→
03

Group Discussion & Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates are invited for a GD + PI round with the admissions panel to assess communication, aptitude & fit.

→
04

Admission Offer & Fee Payment

Selected candidates receive their offer letter and must complete fee payment within the stipulated deadline to confirm their seat.

Frequently Asked Questions

A bachelor's degree in any discipline with a minimum of 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST). Candidates must also have a valid score from CAT, MAT, KMAT, XAT, or any other AICTE-approved management entrance test.
It is a 2-year (4-semester), Full-Time, Residential programme based at the LEAD campus in Dhoni, Palakkad, Kerala. The residential model ensures a highly immersive academic experience with peer learning, industry interactions, and 24/7 academic support.
For six consecutive batches, every eligible and prepared student at LEAD has received a placement offer. The 2024–26 batch achieved a highest package of ₹21.67 LPA, a median CTC of ₹5.0 LPA, and 219+ companies visited campus. Average placement rate: 95%+.
LEAD MBA offers 4 specialisations: Marketing, Finance, Human Resources (HR), and Analytics. Students choose their specialisation at the end of the first year based on aptitude, interest and industry demand.
Yes. LEAD College offers merit scholarships, government fee concessions (SC/ST/OBC), and education loan support through partner banks. Admitted students can contact the finance office for guidance on government scholarship schemes (HDFC Credila, SBI Scholar Loan, etc.).
