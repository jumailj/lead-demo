ABOUT THE PROGRAMME

Computing for
a changing world.

LEAD’s Department of Computer Applications has developed its MCA for the changing needs of technology and society. The programme builds on the department’s academic foundation and was redesigned with autonomous status in 2024.

Students develop technical capability through computing fundamentals, modern technologies and application-oriented work in a fully residential learning environment in Dhoni, Palakkad.
