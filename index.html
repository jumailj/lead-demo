The Admissions Process

A transparent, merit-based selection process for the LEAD MBA 2026 Batch

01

Register & Apply Online

Fill in your application form at admission.lead.ac.in with basic details, academic records, and work experience (if any).

→
02

Entrance Test (CAT / KMAT / MAT / XAT)

Submit valid scores from any national management entrance test. LEAD also accepts Kerala state entrance scores.

→
03

Group Discussion & Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates are invited for a GD + PI round with the admissions panel to assess communication, aptitude & fit.

→
04

Admission Offer & Fee Payment

Selected candidates receive their offer letter and must complete fee payment within the stipulated deadline to confirm their seat.
